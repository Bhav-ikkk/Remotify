## Stage contract

| # | Stage | Input | Output | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Scrape | HTTP/JSON | Raw jobs | `scrapers/*` |
| 2 | Normalize | Raw | Zod job | `normalizers/index.js` |
| 3 | Dedupe | Batch | Unique | `utils/deduplicate.js` |
| 4 | Quality | Unique | Ranked subset | `utils/job-quality.js` |
| 5 | Cap | Ranked | ≤ max (cron ≤12) | `pipeline.js` |
| 6 | AI | Capped + profile brief | Scores | `ai-batch` + Gemini |
| 7 | Persist | Scored | `jobs` rows | Prisma upsert `applyUrl` |
| 8 | Notify | ≥ min_match, not notified | Telegram + PDF | `notification.js` |
| 9 | Enqueue | ≥ apply_min | `applications` | `apply/queue.js` |
| 10 | Claim | queued | preparing + identity | `/api/apply/claim` |
| 11 | Apply | Browser | Outcome | worker adapters |
| 12 | Report | Outcome | Final status | `/api/apply/report` |

## Job identity

- **Primary unique key:** `applyUrl`  
- **Dedupe helpers:** normalized URL (strip utm/hash), `company::title`, Jaccard description similarity > 0.9 (same company, 30-day lookback)  
- **Apply dedupe:** `jobId`, `applyUrl`, `normalizeApplyKey(host+path)`

## Profile → score brief

```text
CandidateProfile (+ skills/projects/experience/priorities)
        │
        ▼
buildAiMatchProfile()   // services/profile.js
        │
        ▼
AIService.evaluateJob(job, brief)
        │
        ▼
{ score, matchedSkills, missingSkills, reason }
```

## Cron vs manual differences

| | Scheduled | Manual override |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Max jobs | min(setting, **12**) | `max_jobs` (default 200) |
| AI delay | 400ms | 1000ms |
| AI budget | 40s wall clock | until finished |
| Scheduler enabled required | yes | no |
