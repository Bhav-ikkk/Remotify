## What the product does

Remotify is a self-hostable remote-job pipeline that:

1. Scrapes boards and public ATS APIs  
2. Normalizes + deduplicates jobs  
3. Quality-filters titles before Gemini spend  
4. Scores fit against your Postgres candidate profile  
5. Sends Telegram alerts with tailored ATS resume PDFs  
6. Queues high-fit Greenhouse / Lever / Ashby applications  
7. Applies via a **local Playwright worker** on your machine  
8. Tracks every attempt in an Application CRM  

## What it deliberately refuses

- LinkedIn / Indeed session bots  
- Fake-submitting unknown ATS portals  
- Inventing resume facts during tailor  
- Running browsers on Vercel Hobby
